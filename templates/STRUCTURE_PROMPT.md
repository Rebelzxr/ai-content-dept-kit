# Structure prompt — learn from a post without copying it

Use this when you find a post that works and want to learn its shape, not steal its words.
The AI breaks down the structure, then tells you what **you** must prepare from your own experience.
It must not invent results or numbers for you.

## 中文版（原版）

```text
下面是一篇别人写的帖子。
不要改写它，也不要抄它的句子。请只帮我拆出它的「结构」：
1. 开头用了什么：痛点、结果，还是问题？
2. 有没有路线图（告诉读者会学到什么）？
3. 步骤怎么排？
4. 有没有写局限或坑？
5. 结尾怎么收？
最后列出：如果我要用自己的经验写同一个题目，我需要自己准备哪些实测、截图或例子。不要替我编任何结果或数字。

帖子：
「<把帖子贴在这里>」
```

## English version

```text
Below is a post written by someone else.
Do not rewrite it, and do not copy its sentences. Only break down its structure:
1. What does the opening use: a pain point, a result, or a question?
2. Is there a roadmap (telling the reader what they will learn)?
3. How are the steps ordered?
4. Does it mention limits or pitfalls?
5. How does it end?
Finally, list what I would need to prepare from my own experience to write on the same topic:
which real tests, screenshots or examples. Do not invent any results or numbers for me.

Post:
"<paste the post here>"
```

## Rules for using it

- Paste the post into your private chat with the AI only. Do not republish someone else's text.
- Use the structure list as a checklist. Fill it with your own tests and screenshots.
- If the AI starts writing a version of the post for you, stop it and repeat: "structure only".
